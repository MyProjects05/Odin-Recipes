Khinkali

Description

Khinkali is a large, savory Georgian dumpling made of twisted knots of dough, traditionally stuffed with a spiced mixture of minced meat and a rich, hot broth. Originating in the rugged Caucasian mountain regions of Pshavi, Mtiuleti, and Khevsureti, it has evolved into a beloved national staple and a cornerstone of Georgian culinary culture.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Water
  • Pepper
  • Meat
  • Onions
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Chili Flakes
  • Fresh herbs

Steps

  1. Make the Dough
  2. Prepare the Filling
  3. Roll and Fold
  4. Boil the Dumplings
