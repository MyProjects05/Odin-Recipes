Pizza

Description

Pizza is a globally renowned Italian dish consisting of a flat, leavened wheat-based dough base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and various optional ingredients, which is then baked at high temperatures. Originating as a humble working-class food in Naples, Italy, it has evolved into one of the world's most versatile and popular culinary staples.
