Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked casserole dish made by stacking wide, flat pasta sheets alternating with layers of rich sauces, cheeses, and seasonings. When baked, the ingredients meld together into a hearty, comforting meal featuring a golden, bubbling, and crispy cheese topping. The word "lasagna" refers to both the individual flat noodle sheets and the finished, layered dish itself.

Ingredients

  • A rich meat sauce
  • A creamy cheese filling
  • Tender pasta sheets

Steps

  1. Prep Sauce: Brown 1 lb ground beef, add onion and garlic, then simmer with tomato sauce and herbs.
  2. Prep Cheese: Mix 15 oz ricotta cheese with 1 egg and ½ cup Parmesan cheese.
  3. Assemble Sauce: Spread a thin layer on the bottom of a 9x13 dish.
  4. Assemble Layers: Repeat layers of noodles, ricotta mix, meat sauce, and mozzarella cheese.
  5. Assemble Top: End with noodles, remaining sauce, and a heavy layer of mozzarella and Parmesan.
  6. Bake Covered: Bake at 375°F (190°C) with aluminum foil for 30–45 minutes.
  7. Bake Uncovered: Remove foil and bake 10-15 minutes until bubbly.
  8. Bake Rest: Cool for 15 minutes before cutting.
